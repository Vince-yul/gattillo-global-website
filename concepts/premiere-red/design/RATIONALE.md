# Gattillo Global redesign rationale

## Concept
House lights down, marquee on. Entertainment is a dark room with a lit sign. The whole site is the dark house: near-black stage, bone type, and exactly one accent, marquee amber. The amber only appears where light would actually be: the bulb rules, the ticker strip, hover states, the glow horizon. The brand behaves like the product it sells.

## Job to be done
Convince me to trust you. The audience is operators, distributors, manufacturers, and IP holders at Fortune 500 scale (Six Flags tier). They are not impulse buyers; they are checking whether Gattillo is a serious counterparty. So the site leads with standards and process, holds a confident quiet register, and never begs. One CTA: talk to us.

## What was wrong before
1. Three identical 3-up card grids in a row. Every section read the same.
2. Cream paper palette read like a SaaS template, not entertainment.
3. Hero was floating dots plus text; no presence.
4. A 1.5 second letter-flicker loader in front of a static page.

## The fixes
- Loader deleted. The page opens with a fast lights-up entrance (glow brightens, type rises). Zero wait.
- Section systems are all different: The Standard is full-width numbered editorial rows; The Playbook is a three-gate progression on a lit connector line; What We Bring is a sticky-split ledger. No cards anywhere.
- Hero is a full-viewport marquee moment: giant Satoshi 900 stack, "entertainment" set as an outlined stroke with a gold period, amber horizon glow beneath, a chase-light bulb rule as the divider.
- The ticker survives (it earns its place) but flips to a gold marquee strip with black type.

## Typography
Satoshi 900 display at up to 158px, tracking -0.03em. General Sans 400/500 body. Two families, left-aligned throughout, no italics.

## Numbering
Plain numerals (1, 2, 3), no leading zeros.
