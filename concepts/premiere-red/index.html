How We Work

Who We Are

The name behind the name.

Tony Gattillo

Founder & CEO

For more than 28 years, Tony Gattillo has been helping grow the amusement industry by building lasting relationships, opening new markets, and delivering innovative entertainment solutions.

As the founder of Gattillo Global, Tony represents some of the world's leading amusement brands while launching his own line of original coin-operated games and attractions. His mission is simple: create products people love to play and help distributors and operators build more profitable entertainment destinations.

With a global network, decades of experience, and a passion for innovation, Tony is committed to powering the next generation of entertainment.
