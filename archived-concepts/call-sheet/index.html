What We Actually Do

Strong properties. Clear opportunities. Commercial outcomes.

Gattillo Global develops and structures entertainment and amusement opportunities built around recognizable intellectual property, talent, and proven market demand.

We work between the organizations that own the audience and the organizations capable of building the experience.

That can mean helping a rights holder bring its intellectual property into a new category, helping an operator develop an attraction audiences will immediately recognize, or assembling the commercial, manufacturing, distribution, and financing partners needed to move a project forward. Our role changes with the opportunity, but the objective remains the same: turn strong entertainment properties into experiences that audiences want and businesses can successfully operate.

I

Opportunity development

We identify where recognized intellectual property, talent, or entertainment brands can create new commercial value.

This begins with the market, not with a product looking for somewhere to go. We study operator needs, audience behaviour, category performance, available rights, and the competitive landscape before recommending a direction.

II

IP, talent and partnership development

We connect the right rights holders, athletes, talent, operators, manufacturers, distributors, and commercial partners around a clearly defined opportunity.

We help establish the partnership structure, responsibilities, approvals, commercial terms, and long-term objectives required to keep every party aligned.

III

Commercial strategy and deal structure

A compelling concept is not enough. The economics must work.

Gattillo helps shape the business model behind each opportunity, including licensing, ownership, revenue participation, manufacturing costs, distribution, financing, and routes to market.

Our goal is to build partnerships that create lasting enterprise value, not one-time transactions.

IV

Development and execution

Once the opportunity and economics are validated, we help coordinate the specialists responsible for bringing it to market.

That may include concept development, product design, licensing approvals, engineering, manufacturing, distribution, operator placement, launch planning, and international expansion.

Gattillo does not replace these specialists. We bring them together, establish the commercial framework, and keep the project moving toward the same outcome.

V

Growth and expansion

The work does not stop when an experience reaches the floor.

We look at how successful concepts can expand into additional locations, territories, formats, products, and partnerships. Every project should have the potential to become more valuable over time.

Our Process

From opportunity to operating experience.

Every project is different, but the discipline behind it remains consistent.

I

Find the opportunity

We begin by identifying a real opening in the market.

That may come from an operator looking for a recognizable attraction, a rights holder exploring a new category, a manufacturer with an exceptional capability, or a partner with access to an underserved territory.

We define the audience, the commercial opportunity, and the reason the project should exist.

II

Validate demand

Before significant development begins, we speak with the people responsible for buying, placing, and operating the experience.

We determine what audiences are responding to, what operators need, what price points are realistic, how the experience will perform on the floor, and whether there is a credible path to scale.

III

Assemble the right partners

Once the opportunity is clear, we bring together the organizations capable of delivering it.

Depending on the project, that can include studios, leagues, athletes, talent, operators, designers, manufacturers, distributors, investors, and regional partners.

Every participant is selected for a specific reason. No unnecessary layers. No introductions without a plan.

IV

Secure the rights and structure the deal

We work with the appropriate parties to establish licensing, territory, approvals, ownership, revenue participation, manufacturing responsibilities, financing, and distribution.

Before development moves forward, every partner should understand the opportunity, the economics, and their role in delivering it.

V

Build and prepare for market

With the rights and commercial model established, our development and manufacturing partners turn the opportunity into a finished experience.

Gattillo helps coordinate the process across concept development, design, approvals, production, pricing, logistics, and operator readiness.

VI

Launch, learn and scale

We support the route to market, from initial placement through broader commercial expansion.

Performance is evaluated through audience response, operator economics, repeatability, and long-term growth potential. What works can then be expanded across locations, territories, formats, and future partnerships.
